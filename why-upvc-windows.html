Buying guide

Why uPVC windows and doors suit Haryana homes.

Dust storms, 45°C summers, monsoon damp and busy roads are hard on wood and iron. Here is how uPVC copes, and when aluminium is the better choice.

Dust and heat

Keeping dust and summer heat out.

A uPVC window closes against rubber gaskets on every side, so far less fine dust gets in during an aandhi than through old wooden or iron frames, which warp and leave gaps.

uPVC is also a poor conductor of heat. Its hollow, multi-chamber profile holds back heat far better than an iron or plain aluminium frame, and with double glass the room stays noticeably cooler and the AC has less work to do.

Compared with wood and iron

Built for local weather, year after year.

No termites, no rust

uPVC does not rot, swell in the monsoon, or attract termites the way wood does, and it cannot rust like iron grills and frames. Steel reinforcement inside the profile is galvanised and sealed away from moisture.

Quieter rooms

Tight seals and double glass cut down traffic, market and highway noise. Casement uPVC windows seal the tightest, so they are the first choice for bedrooms facing a road.

Low maintenance

No painting, polishing or anti-rust treatment. Wipe the frames with soapy water and keep the tracks clean, and the finish stays bright for years.

Sliding, casement or aluminium?

Choosing the right system for each room.

Sliding uPVC windows save space and suit wide openings in living rooms and bedrooms. Casement uPVC windows open fully for airflow and seal best against dust and noise, which makes them ideal for kitchens, bathrooms and road-facing rooms.

For very large openings, shopfronts or a slim modern look, aluminium sliding doors or aluminium system windows can be the better fit. We make both, so we can recommend honestly.

What do uPVC windows cost in Bhuna and Fatehabad?

What affects the price of a uPVC window?

Four things set the price: the size of the opening, the profile series, single or double glass, and the hardware such as handles, locks and rollers. A casement window usually costs a little more than a sliding window of the same size because of its hinges and multi-point locks.

Are uPVC windows more expensive than wood or iron?

The upfront cost is often similar to good-quality wooden frames. Over the years uPVC usually works out cheaper because it needs no painting, termite treatment or repairs for warping and rust.
