Price and product questions

Choosing uPVC or aluminium windows and doors.

What decides the price of uPVC windows and doors?

The price of uPVC windows and doors depends on the opening size, the profile series, single or double glass, and the hardware. Share your sizes on call or WhatsApp and we will send a clear written quote.

How do aluminium windows and aluminium system windows differ in price?

Standard aluminium sliding windows are the more economical option. Aluminium system windows use engineered slimline profiles, gaskets, and multi-point hardware, so they cost more but look slimmer and seal better.

Casement or sliding uPVC windows: which should I choose?

Sliding uPVC windows save space and suit wide openings. Casement uPVC windows open fully for airflow and seal more tightly against dust and noise, which makes them popular for kitchens and bathrooms.

Do you manufacture the windows or deal in branded systems?

Both. We manufacture uPVC and aluminium windows and doors in our Bhuna workshop and also supply branded systems. Our own team measures and installs across our service areas.

Why choose uPVC over wood or iron?

uPVC keeps out dust and heat, does not rot, rust or attract termites, and needs no painting. Read our guide to uPVC windows for Haryana homes.
